Services

From capacity assessment to construction-ready packages

Planning and engineering support for carriers, contractors and engineering firms, across the full OSP lifecycle.

01

Fiber Capacity Planning & Analysis

F1 feeder and distribution fiber capacity assessments that determine network capacity needs, identify fiber shortages and constraints, and produce reinforcement recommendations before the engineering/design phase.

02

Route Tracing & Network Records Review

Review of network records and systems to trace existing routes and infrastructure, evaluate fiber availability, and find capacity gaps within Local/ILEC-style networks.

03

OSP / FTTX Design (GPON)

GPON backbone design, fiber pathway routing and assignment, distribution layouts, terminal placements, cabinet configurations, equipment sizing, fiber routes, splicing prints and work orders.

04

Fiber-to-the-Business & Customer-Driven Fiber

Capacity analysis, route verification and construction-ready planning packages supporting Fiber-to-the-Business deployments, network expansion and customer-driven fiber growth.

05

Aerial, Underground & Buried Plant

Conduit, vault and pathway analysis and planning; support for cable relocation, upgrades and reinforcement of underground and aerial plant.

06

QA/QC & Constructability Reviews

Detailed capacity and constructability reviews of OSP/ISP designs, QA/QC of construction drawings and documents, and as-built documentation.

07

Cost Estimating & Planning Packages

Project budgets, cost estimates and quotes based on labor, materials and regional construction costs; cost tracking and billable work packages; planning packages ready for Engineering handoff.

08

Permitting & Utility Coordination

Permit and pathway approval coordination with cities and utilities, including AT&T duct and pole attachments, plus coordination with construction crews, municipalities and customers to resolve field issues.

09

Field Assessments & Project Management

Nationwide site assessments, route verification and field data collection; schedule, ECD and deliverable management; Scope of Work review; CAD drawings and engineering documentation.
