Services

What our team delivers

Planning, design and field measurement services for fiber networks, from capacity review through permit-ready drawings.

OSP Fiber Design

Aerial and underground fiber routes, extensions and relocations: route selection, cable sizing, splice and terminal locations, splicing prints and work orders, drawn to the owner's standards.

F1 / Feeder & Distribution Planning

Feeder and distribution capacity assessments, route tracing in network records, fiber-shortage identification and reinforcement recommendations before detailed design.

GPON / OLT Network Design

FTTH and FTTX distribution on a GPON architecture: terminal placements, cabinet configurations, equipment sizing and fiber assignments, planned around OLT and PON capacity.

Pole Fielding

Pole fielding and route walkouts anywhere in Southern California: pole data, attachment heights, photos and ground-level measurements that feed accurate design.

Pole Loading

Pole loading and attachment analysis support for new aerial fiber: existing conditions, clearances and added load, documented for the pole owner's attachment application.

JPA & Joint Use

Joint pole association (JPA), joint use and pole attachment coordination between pole owners, attachers, utilities and cities, so everyone knows what moves and when.

Permitting & Make-Ready Support

Make-ready documentation, pole attachment applications and permit drawings, with submittals and corrections coordinated with cities and utilities through approval.

Contract & Overflow Capacity

Added planning and design capacity when your backlog grows, and scaled back when it slows, without adding headcount on your side.

FAQ

Common questions

What does OSP mean?

OSP stands for outside plant: the fiber cables, conduit, poles, vaults and handholes outside of buildings that carry a network from the central office or hub to homes and businesses.

Where do you work?

We are based in Fontana and do pole fielding and walkouts anywhere in Southern California. Design and planning work can also be delivered remotely.

Do you do construction or climb poles?

No. We are a design and engineering firm. Our field work is pole fielding and ground-level measurements; we do not do construction, climbing or line work.

Can we start with a small job?

Yes. Many teams start with one package so they can review our work before sending more.

How is pricing set?

Per project or hourly, quoted after we review the scope. You get the rate and schedule in writing before work starts.

What files will we receive?

PDF drawing sets and AutoCAD (DWG) files, plus supporting documents in the format your team uses. We can work to your title blocks, layers and design standards.
